# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Business Analysts

### Role Summary
Business Analysts translate business needs into clear requirements and workflows so the team can plan and deliver the right solution.

### Responsibilities
- Elicit and document business and user needs
- Define workflows, requirements, and acceptance criteria with the Product Manager
- Identify gaps, assumptions, and impacts across processes
- Validate that delivered changes address the agreed needs

### Goals
- Make requirements clear, testable, and aligned with business outcomes
- Reduce rework caused by ambiguity or missed dependencies

### Collaboration with existing roles
- Work with the Project Manager on requirements dependencies and decisions that affect scope or schedule
- Partner with the Product Manager to refine priorities and acceptance criteria
- Answer Developers' questions and clarify requirements for QA/Testing
- Gather feedback from Stakeholders and confirm that proposed workflows meet their needs

### Typical Communication
- Requirements and process-flow documentation
- Backlog refinement and stakeholder discovery sessions
- Acceptance-criteria reviews with product and delivery teams

---

## Scrum Masters

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters facilitate the team's agreed agile practices, help remove impediments, and support continuous improvement without owning product scope or project commitments.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint planning, daily standups, reviews, and retrospectives
- Help the team identify and resolve or escalate impediments
- Support healthy team collaboration and consistent use of working agreements
- Surface delivery risks and improvement actions

### Goals
- Help the team work effectively and sustainably
- Improve transparency and flow while protecting the team's focus

### Collaboration with existing roles
- Coordinate with the Project Manager on impediments, dependencies, and delivery visibility while keeping schedule and scope decisions with the appropriate owners
- Help the Product Manager prepare and clarify prioritized backlog items
- Facilitate Developers' planning and collaboration, including coordination with QA/Testing
- Make progress and impediments visible to Stakeholders through agreed reviews and updates

### Typical Communication
- Sprint ceremonies and team working agreements
- Impediment tracking and retrospective action items
- Sprint progress and improvement updates

---

## QA / Test Leads

### Role Summary
QA / Test Leads coordinate the test approach and quality evidence so the team can identify defects and assess whether work is ready to release.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy, coverage, and quality risks with the team
- Coordinate functional, integration, and regression testing
- Track defects and communicate their impact and status
- Confirm test results against acceptance criteria and the Definition of Done

### Goals
- Find important issues early and provide transparent release-readiness evidence
- Help the team deliver reliable changes that meet agreed requirements

### Collaboration with existing roles
- Keep the Project Manager informed of test progress, quality risks, and release blockers
- Work with the Product Manager and Business Analyst to make acceptance criteria testable
- Partner with Developers on testability, defect triage, and fixes
- Coordinate QA/Testing contributors and share clear test outcomes and open risks with Stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Test plans, test results, and defect reports
- Quality and readiness reviews with the delivery team
- Release-blocker escalations

---

## Release Managers

### Role Summary
Release Managers coordinate release readiness and deployment communication, ensuring required checks, approvals, and handoffs are completed.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain the release schedule and coordinate release activities
- Track readiness criteria, dependencies, and outstanding blockers
- Coordinate release notes, deployment plans, and rollback or mitigation information
- Confirm post-deployment verification and capture release outcomes

### Goals
- Make releases predictable, traceable, and low risk
- Ensure teams and affected users know what is changing and when

### Collaboration with existing roles
- Coordinate the release timeline, dependencies, and status with the Project Manager
- Confirm scope and customer-facing changes with the Product Manager
- Align Developers and QA/Testing on build, verification, and rollback readiness
- Coordinate deployment notices and support handoffs with Stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Release plans and readiness checklists
- Deployment coordination and go/no-go updates
- Release notes and post-release summaries

---

## Support / Operations Representatives

### Role Summary
Support / Operations Representatives bring production support and operational expertise into planning, release, and incident response.

### Responsibilities
- Identify operational requirements, support impacts, and monitoring needs
- Review runbooks, escalation paths, and readiness for operational handoff
- Share recurring customer issues and production feedback with the team
- Support incident triage and communicate operational impact

### Goals
- Keep services supportable and reliable after changes reach production
- Shorten the path from user-reported issues to actionable improvements

### Collaboration with existing roles
- Advise the Project Manager on operational dependencies, support capacity, and risks
- Share customer and service insights with the Product Manager to inform priorities
- Work with Developers and QA/Testing on observability, runbooks, and incident fixes
- Coordinate support readiness and incident communications with Stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Operational readiness reviews and runbooks
- Incident reports and service-health updates
- Support trends and feedback summaries

---

## Technical Writers

### Role Summary
Technical Writers create and maintain user-facing and operational documentation so people can understand, adopt, and support delivered changes.

### Responsibilities
- Draft and update user guides, help content, and technical documentation
- Identify documentation needs and owners during planning
- Verify documentation is accurate and accessible before release
- Coordinate documentation updates with release communications

### Goals
- Make product and process information accurate, findable, and useful
- Keep documentation aligned with the behavior of released changes

### Collaboration with existing roles
- Coordinate documentation scope and timing with the Project Manager
- Confirm user needs, terminology, and messaging with the Product Manager
- Work with Developers and QA/Testing to verify technical details and changed behavior
- Gather feedback from Stakeholders and align published guidance with release and support teams

### Typical Communication
- Documentation reviews and content checklists
- User guides, release notes, and knowledge-base updates
- Feedback requests to product and engineering teams
